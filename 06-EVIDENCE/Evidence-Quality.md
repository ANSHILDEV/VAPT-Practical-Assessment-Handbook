# Evidence Quality Checklist

A strong finding explains:
1. Asset/function affected.
2. Exact request/action that triggers it.
3. Account role used.
4. Actual application behaviour.
5. Why it is a security weakness.
6. Impact actually demonstrated.
7. Reproduction steps.
8. Redactions performed.
9. What could not be tested.

A scanner alert alone is not sufficient evidence.
