# Severity Guide

Use the client's rating method if supplied. Otherwise consider:
- Critical: severe compromise of sensitive systems/data or major business function with realistic exploitability.
- High: significant unauthorised access, sensitive data exposure or control bypass.
- Medium: meaningful weakness requiring conditions or with limited impact.
- Low: limited impact or defence-in-depth weakness.
- Informational: observation without demonstrated direct security impact.

Consider exploitability, privileges, user interaction, scope, confidentiality, integrity, availability and banking context. Do not copy scanner severity blindly. If CVSS is required, state version, vector and context.
