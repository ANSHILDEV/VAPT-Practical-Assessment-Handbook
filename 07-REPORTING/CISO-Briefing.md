# CISO Briefing – 5 Minute Structure

1. **Purpose and scope:** what was tested, when and under what authorisation.
2. **Overall result:** severity counts and important limitations.
3. **Top risks:** affected business function, realistic attacker action, evidence, business consequence and recommended action.
4. **Immediate actions:** containment/short-term mitigation, then permanent remediation.
5. **Next steps:** owners, retesting and untested areas.

Speak in business impact, not only payloads or tool names. Separate confirmed findings from observations and limitations. Say what was assessed and found; do not claim “no vulnerabilities”.
