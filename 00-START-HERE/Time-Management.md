# Time Management

Adapt to the time actually provided.

| Stage | Suggested share | Output |
|---|---:|---|
| Scope/setup | 5–10% | Scope sheet and tools ready |
| Recon/mapping | 15–20% | Asset and endpoint inventory |
| Manual testing | 40–50% | Candidate findings |
| Evidence/retest | 10–15% | Reproducible evidence |
| Reporting/review | 15–20% | Final report and briefing |

Prioritise authentication, authorisation, sensitive data exposure, injection and banking business logic. A few well-evidenced findings are better than many unverified alerts.
